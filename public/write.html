Email your state rep and senator. It takes a minute.

A note from a voter in their own district is the thing legislators actually read. The letter below is written. Look up who represents you, add your name, and send it from your own email app.

  1. Find your two legislators

    Every Texan has one state representative and one state senator. The state's lookup tool takes an address and gives you their names and email links. It opens in a new tab.

    Open Who Represents Me

    Their email addresses follow a pattern: firstname.lastname@house.texas.gov for representatives and firstname.lastname@senate.texas.gov for senators. Each member's page on the lookup tool has a contact link if the pattern doesn't work.

  2. Add your name and where you live

  3. Send the letter

    Edit anything you want. A line about your own commute makes it land harder.

    If nothing opens, your browser isn't linked to an email app. Copy the letter and paste it into Gmail, Outlook or whatever you use.
