About this project

An independent look at who controls public infrastructure in Texas, starting with the toll lanes North Texans drive every day.

Who we are

Started by a North Texas driver who got tired of paying surge tolls without knowing where the money went.

  • No company money
  • No party
  • No campaign

How we check facts

  1. Traceable or it doesn't run

    Every figure comes from a government record, a company's own filings or statements, or an established news outlet.

  2. Every number is linked

    Tap any small number on the site to see its source. See all sources.

  3. Dated and updated

    Ownership stakes can change through sales that aren't always announced. We note the date of each figure and update it when newer filings appear.

  4. Both sides

    These contracts rebuilt free lanes, moved financial risk off the state and return the roads to Texas in 2061. Drivers deserve the full picture, not just the parts that make people angry.

Privacy

  • Your name and ZIP, only with your own state legislators when we deliver the petition

We never

  • Sell, rent or share your email
  • Email you unless you asked for updates
  • Use advertising trackers or analytics cookies
