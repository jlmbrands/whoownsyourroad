10,000 names before the Legislature opens

The 90th Texas Legislature convenes in 87 days. Signatures are grouped by ZIP code so every representative and senator sees how many of their own constituents signed.

To the members of the Texas House and Senate:

The express lanes on I-35W, I-820, SH 121/183, I-635 and I-35E are on state highways, but private concession companies set the prices and keep the tolls until 2061. Rush-hour tolls on these lanes have been posted above $20.1213 Drivers deserve a limit on what they can be charged and a clear view of where the money goes. We ask you to:

  • Put a hard ceiling on surge tolls

    Set a maximum per-mile toll in state law for privately operated managed lanes, with no contract exception that lets prices run past it.

  • Open the books

    Require each concession company to publish yearly toll revenue, operating costs, debt payments, payouts to investors, and the share returned to the state, road by road.

  • Price the exit

    Direct TxDOT to publish what it would cost to end each contract early, so the public can weigh a buyback against 35 more years of private tolling.

  • No new deals without a vote

    Require a recorded vote of the Legislature before TxDOT signs or extends any private toll concession on a state highway.
