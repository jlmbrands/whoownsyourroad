Work ahead

The Legislature meets in 87 days.

We're asking Texas lawmakers for four things:

  1. Cap the price

    A hard limit on surge tolls, written into state law. No exceptions in the contract.

  2. Open the books

    Every toll company publishes what it collects, what it spends, and what investors take home. Every year, every road.

  3. Price the exit

    TxDOT publishes what it would cost to end each contract early, so Texans can decide if it's worth it.

  4. No new deals without a vote

    Lawmakers must vote before TxDOT signs or extends any private toll contract.

To be fair: the deals rebuilt the free lanes too, and if traffic falls short the investors lose money, not the state.121 None of that requires 35 more years with no price limit and no public books.
